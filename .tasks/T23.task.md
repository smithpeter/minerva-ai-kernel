## Goal

Show how agent and SDK consumers can feed a structured failure observation into
Minerva and consume the decision without bypassing policy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Add at least one minimal Python SDK example that builds or loads an
  observation and obtains a decision.
- Add at least one agent-tool failure example or guide that maps a failed tool
  call to a Minerva observation.
- Document that decisions are advisory until policy allows them.
- Keep examples local, redacted, and free of external service credentials.
- Existing compile, unit tests, and eval smoke pass.

## Non-Goals

- Do not implement adapters for every framework.
- Do not require network access or a remote LLM.
- Do not execute Minerva-proposed actions automatically.
- Do not use VoxSign state, tasks, tools, or docs.
