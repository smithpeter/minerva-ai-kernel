## Acceptance Criteria

- It builds a redacted prompt and obtains a `Decision` through the provider abstraction.

- CLI output includes failure, action, confidence, risk, escalation, policy decision reason, and redaction summary when present.
- Tests cover successful diagnosis, invalid input, policy-blocked decisions, and redaction summary output.
